# Biceps Curl Technique, Biomechanics and Form Correction Guide
**Source:** NSCA Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning (4th Ed.) & Marcolin et al. (2018)
**Citation:** Marcolin, G., et al. (2018). Differences in electromyographic activity of biceps brachii during barbell vs dumbbell curls. Journal of Sports Science & Medicine, 17(3), 475-483.

## 1. Biomechanics of the Biceps Curl
The biceps curl is an open-chain isolation exercise targeting the biceps brachii (long and short heads), brachialis, and brachioradialis.
Key joint angles:
- **Starting / Lowered Phase:** Elbow angle is extended at 160° to 180°. Upper arms remain stationary and perpendicular to the floor.
- **Peak Contraction / Up Phase:** Elbow angle flexes to < 50° with full forearm supination.

## 2. Common Form Errors
### Error A: Torso Swinging / Momentum ("SWINGING")
- **Biomechanical Cause:** The trunk tilts backward > 15° from the vertical plane during the concentric phase.
- **Root Causes:** Lifting excessive weight, using lumbar hyperextension and hip thrust to bypass the sticking point.
- **Risk:** Strain on the lower lumbar spine and drastically diminished biceps muscle tension.
- **Correction:** Keep knees slightly soft, tuck the pelvis, brace the core, and pin shoulder blades back. The torso should remain completely static throughout the curl.

### Error B: Elbow Drifting Forward / Outward ("ELBOW DRIFTING")
- **Biomechanical Cause:** Elbow joint moves forward relative to the shoulder seam (drift > 0.06 normalized units).
- **Impact:** Involves the anterior deltoid to assist the lift, shortening the moment arm on the elbow and robbing the biceps of peak tension at the top.
- **Correction:** Imagine elbows are glued or bolted to your ribcage. Only forearms should rotate around the stationary elbow pivot.

## 3. Recommended Camera Setup for Biceps Curls
- **Angle:** Frontal or 45-degree angle.
- **Distance:** 1.5 to 2.5 meters.
- **Framing:** Should capture from mid-thigh up through the top of the head so both upper arm alignment and torso sway can be detected.
